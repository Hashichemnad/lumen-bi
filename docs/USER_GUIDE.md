# Lumen guide

Lumen shows your organisation's numbers as dashboards and charts. You only ever see the data you are
allowed to see. Lumen works in English and Arabic (My account → Appearance).

## Finding things

- **Home**: your favourite dashboards (the ☆ on any dashboard), recent items, and search (Ctrl K).
- **Workspaces** (top of the menu): pick a department to see only its dashboards and charts.
- **Ask your data** (Home, if your organisation has AI): type a question such as *"sales by region this
  year"* or *"المبيعات حسب الإمارة"*. You get a chart and a short answer from the data you may see; save it
  or open it in the chart builder.

## Using a dashboard

- **Filters** at the top apply to every chart. **Click a bar or point** to filter the other charts, drill
  down (e.g. year → month), view the rows behind it, or add a note.
- A tile's **⋮** menu: view data, export to Excel or CSV, save as a picture, **Explain** (what stands out).
- **Export** (top): the whole dashboard as PDF or a picture.
- **Present** and **TV mode**: full-screen, one section at a time; TV mode cycles and refreshes by itself.
- **More → Subscribe**: receive the dashboard by email on a schedule. **More → Alerts**: a message when a
  value crosses a limit, something unusual happens, or a KPI goes off track. Each person always gets their
  own data.
- **Comments** (speech bubble): discuss the dashboard with colleagues.

## Building (Explorers and Creators)

- **Charts → New chart**: choose the data, drag fields to Columns and Rows (dimensions split, measures add
  up), pick a chart in Show Me. Or type what you want in *Describe the chart you want*. Options include a
  trend line, reference line and **Forecast** for charts over dates. Guide: [CHARTS.md](CHARTS.md).
- **Dashboards → New dashboard** (or **Build with AI**): add charts, text, pictures and filters; arrange
  tiles by dragging; share with people, groups or a workspace. Guide: [DASHBOARDS.md](DASHBOARDS.md).
- **Data** (Creators): connect systems, model the data, set who sees what. Guides:
  [CONNECTORS.md](CONNECTORS.md), [MODELLING.md](MODELLING.md), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Your account

- **Two-factor sign-in**: add a code from an authenticator app to your password. Keep the recovery codes;
  each one signs you in once if you lose your phone.
- **Daily summary**: a morning email about the KPIs on your favourite dashboards.
- **Connected AI apps**: Claude or other apps you allowed to read Lumen for you; disconnect them here.
- **Sign out on all devices** if you lost a laptop or phone.

## Getting help

Ask your Lumen admin. They can see what a dataset or dashboard is used by, reset your two-factor sign-in, and
check what you can see.
